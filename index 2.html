The Studio

One roof,
every kind of care.

White Orchid was built around a simple idea: a salon shouldn't need separate menus for men, women, and children. Every stylist is trained across hair types, skin types, and age groups — so your family can walk in together and leave together.

We keep the studio calm, the products good, and the consultations honest — if something won't suit you, we'll say so before we start.
